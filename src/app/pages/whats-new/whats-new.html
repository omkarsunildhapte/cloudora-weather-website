How updates reach you

Cloudora Weather updates through Google Play like any other Android app — if you have automatic updates switched on you will simply have the newest version. The app also checks with our servers on startup and will let you know when a newer release is available, so you are never left on an old build without knowing it.

Have something you'd like to see in the next version? We read everything. Send us a note — feature requests genuinely shape what gets built next.
