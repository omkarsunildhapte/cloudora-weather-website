The numbers, explained

A weather app throws a lot of numbers at you: an air quality rating, a UV index, a "feels like" temperature that disagrees with the thermometer, a radar map in five colours. These guides explain what each one is actually measuring — and what you should do about it.

Written by people, not a model

Every figure in these guides comes from a published source — the World Health Organization's air quality guidelines, the WMO/WHO Global Solar UV Index, the national weather services that define the heat index and wind chill, and OpenWeatherMap's own documented index bands, which is the data Cloudora Weather displays. Where meteorology is genuinely uncertain or rule-of-thumb, the guides say so rather than inventing a number.
