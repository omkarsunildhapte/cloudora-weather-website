Stored Only On Your Device

All of the following is kept in the app's local storage and never transmitted to us:

  • App Preferences — onboarding state, display settings, and your last-viewed location.
  • Cached Weather Data — the most recent forecast response, kept so the app can show something useful while it refreshes.
  • Privacy Consent — your choices from the in-app consent dialog (AI processing, notifications).

Sent to Third-Party Services

  • Location Coordinates — to show weather for where you are, your device's latitude and longitude (or the coordinates of a place you search for) are sent to OpenWeatherMap to fetch current conditions, the forecast, air quality, and UV index. No account identifier or other personal data is attached to these requests.
  • Weather Context for AI Insight — when the AI Weather Insight is enabled, a summary of the current forecast (temperature, humidity, wind, rain chance, and the location name) is sent to Google Gemini, falling back to OpenRouter when Gemini is unavailable, so a language model can write the insight. Nothing you type is involved, and you can switch AI processing off in the app's privacy preferences at any time.
  • How These Requests Travel — the app does not contact these providers directly. Requests pass through Cloudora's own server on Cloudflare, which attaches our provider credentials and forwards them. That server sees the coordinates being looked up and, as any web server does, your IP address; it does not log request contents or associate them with an identity.
  • Location (while using the app) — requested so the app can show weather for your current position. If you decline, Cloudora Weather falls back to a default location and still works; you can also search for a place instead. Location is never collected in the background.
  • Notifications — requested only if you opt in to weather alerts. Alerts are generated on your device from the forecast data; there is no push-notification server.
  • Local Storage — used to persist preferences, cached forecasts, and consent choices between sessions.

No other device permissions are requested or used.
  • Fetch and display weather, forecast, and air-quality information for your location.
  • Generate the optional AI Weather Insight for the day.
  • Remember your preferences and consent choices across sessions.

We do not sell, rent, share, or monetize your data in any way, and we do not build a profile of your location history.

OpenWeatherMap Weather Data

Weather, forecast, air-pollution, and UV data are provided by OpenWeatherMap. Requests contain the coordinates being looked up. Review the OpenWeatherMap Privacy Policy to understand how they handle request data.

OpenRouter AI AI Insight Feature

The AI Weather Insight is generated by language models accessed through OpenRouter and its partner model providers. Review the OpenRouter Privacy Policy to understand how they handle prompt data.

Google Gemini AI Insight Feature

The primary model behind the AI Weather Insight. Requests carry the weather summary described above and no personal identifier. Review the Gemini API Terms

Open-Meteo History & UV Data

Past-weather charts and the UV index come from Open-Meteo, which receives the coordinates being looked up. Review the Open-Meteo Terms

RainViewer Radar Imagery

Animated precipitation radar on the map is served by RainViewer. Requests are for map tiles covering a geographic area, not for your exact position. Review the RainViewer Privacy Policy

Esri Base Map

The dark base map underneath the radar is served by Esri's ArcGIS tile service, which receives requests for the map tiles being displayed. Review the Esri Privacy Statement

News Publishers Weather News

The news feed is assembled from the public RSS feeds of the BBC, NPR, The Guardian and DW, retrieved through the rss2json conversion service. Article links open on the publisher's own site, where their privacy policy applies.

Cloudflare Hosting & API Proxy

This site and the API proxy described above run on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare processes request metadata, including IP addresses, to route and protect traffic. Review the Cloudflare Privacy Policy

You have full control over your data:

  • Revoke Location Access — at any time from your device's app settings. The app then falls back to the last location it resolved, or asks you to turn location access back on — it never guesses a place for you.
  • Change Consent — withdraw or update your AI processing and notification choices at any time from inside the app.
  • Delete Everything — use "Delete All My Data" in the app, or uninstall it; all data lives in the app's local storage on your device.
  • GDPR Rights (EU / EEA) — you have rights to access, rectify, erase, and port your data. Contact us for requests.
  • CCPA Rights (California) — you have the right to know what data is collected, delete it, and opt out of its sale (we don't sell data).

Cloudora Weather does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

All local data is protected by your device's operating system security. Transmissions to OpenWeatherMap and OpenRouter use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

The Cloudora Weather app uses on-device local storage, not traditional HTTP cookies. This data stays entirely on your device and is not automatically sent to any server, unlike cookies. You can clear it at any time from within the app.

This website is different: it may use Google Analytics, but only after you accept the consent banner. Decline it and nothing is loaded or stored.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and notify you within the app if the changes are material. Continued use of Cloudora Weather after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
